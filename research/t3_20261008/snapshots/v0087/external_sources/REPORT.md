## Critical technical caution
KO files are raw droplet matrices, whereas E8.5 WT archive contains filtered counts. **Do not use all KO barcodes as cells** or compare raw droplet counts with filtered WT. Libraries a/b are partitions of a pool of embryos, not independent embryo replicates. Recover the study's SNP-based cell-to-embryo and state assignments (Supplementary Table 2) before claiming embryo-held-out validation. Without them, genotype-held-out tests remain possible but cell-level random splits are not biological replication. Supplementary metadata retrieval is ongoing; PMC download endpoint currently serves a proof-of-work page, not an Excel file. No CAPTCHA was solved.

## Objective remaining genotype review
The remaining seven original epigenetic perturbations are **not blanket excluded**. Dnmt1 shows transposon/imprinting defects; G9a/Ehmt2 imprinting/silencing changes; Dnmt3b milder methylation defects; Kmt2b altered CGI protection and delayed differentiation. These are plausible next interventions with distinct mechanisms and require a scoped addition plus file hashes. Eed/Rnf2 primarily overproduce posterior extraembryonic/PGC states and fail neural progression; L3mbtl2 arrests near E7.0. Severe arrest by itself does not establish Gata4/beta-catenin phenocopy. Those three are deferred initially because there may be very little relevant heart-state support, not declared banned. Their utility is best assessed with published cell-state counts before paying the download/processing cost. Cdkn2a single KO and Eed/Cdkn2a double KO are separate rescue controls outside the initial ten and not admitted by this permit.
