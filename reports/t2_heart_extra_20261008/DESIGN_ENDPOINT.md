## Motivation and operator
The five exact source-shared labels cover only about32% of final rows. Older broad crosswalk failed; this route does not map new late CM names to an earlier name. It models continuing maturation toward an independently observed late cardiac endpoint. Source carrier remains unmodified in geometry/composition. Cells are eligible solely if their released latest-stage celltype contains CM (development V-CM/IFT-CM; final V-CM/A-CM/SV-CM/OFT-CM). Other cell states retain the explicit reconstructed median09-library recipe.

1. Restrict external and released data to genuinely measured shared panel genes; absence of a measured gene is never treated as a biological zero. Minimum200 common genes required. Raw external counts are normalized consistently on that common panel and log1p transformed. There is no use of saved external embedding, regulon, classifier or HVG set.
2. Create up to32 endpoint microstates by source-only PCA and kmeans, capped to retain at least8 bins/cells per state. Endpoint state expression is a pseudobulk mean of common-panel normalized raw counts, converted to within-pseudobulk gene percentile ranks. This reduces dropout; rank comparisons avoid direct transfer of assay-specific expression levels.
3. Match each source CM to up to8 endpoint microstates by gene-rank squared distance, with soft weights whose bandwidth is the source row's median neighbor distance. Convert the weighted endpoint ranks into the source cell's own common-gene expression quantiles. Endpoint uses gene ordering, never absolute count levels, cell proportions or spatial coordinates.
4. Use a cubic Hermite bridge from current stage toE14.5: initial derivative is observed same-state median expression difference divided by observed interval; terminal derivative is fixed zero as a conservative regularizing boundary assumption, NOT a claim that cardiac development stops atE14.5. Forecast at actual target time. For newly named CM states absent at earlier stage, initial derivative is zero but endpoint displacement remains. No named-label crosswalk. The Hermite coefficients are fixed by developmental time, not fitted to held-stage outcomes.
5. Common-panel prediction replaces the common genes of the reconstructed recipe; preserve that row's common-panel implied raw library exactly. Genes unmeasured externally remain exactly as in the recipe; source observations determine their changes. This keeps all500 genes, nonnegativity and total library, and leaves source coordinates/row identities exact.
