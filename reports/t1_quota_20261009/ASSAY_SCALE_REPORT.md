## Fit and bounded evidence

Expression, labels and moments used to fit the new classifier and conditional-positive SD slopes come only from official E8.5 and two external E8.5 libraries. External E9.5 measured-gene availability metadata restricts the shared panel; no E9.5 expression or labels enter fitting. E9.5 external labels come from that fresh E8.5-only classifier, and official E9.5 labels only stratify evaluation. Separate early libraries determine support and effective positive counts.

The frozen E9.5 assay gate passes: mean state-wise median log-SD error decreases 15.17%, with 8/9 state wins. The gene-shuffled control is worse. A post-freeze, fixed state-median slope diagnostic also performs worse in all nine evaluated states; it changes neither the gate nor the model. ECTO remains in the source report despite worsening. The target operator retains the original temporal field's seven-group support; BLOOD/ECTO/ENDODERM are excluded by that pre-existing support, not by this assay. EPI lacks early replicate support and stays unchanged; NCC retains its supported source fit despite a small E9.5 diagnostic sample.

This is conditional-positive, confidence-selected cross-study evidence. Whole-cell/nucleus, study, stage and population differences are confounded; the fitted scale is not identified as a pure platform correction. E9.5 transfer does not prove transfer to E14.5 or the hidden future target.

## Operator and nontrivial repair

For each supported fine-state/gene, interpolate the full carrier positive empirical quantiles onto parent positive ranks, then use Qcarrier + b*(Qparent-Qcarrier). Keep exact parent detection support. Empty positive sides have explicit observed-data fallbacks. Use the smallest observed parent/carrier positive as the floor, then stable sorting for monotonicity. Apply the same parameter-free CP10k closure as v0096. Intercepts are not transferred.

The full b=1 endpoint exactly reproduces v0094 and then v0096 after closure. Four unit controls pass, including extrapolation with crossings but no floor violations. The actual parent-bound board validator passes all 5,118 rows and 32,285 genes.

The floor rate is 0.1214%, within the frozen 1% stop rule. However, 20.68% of adjacent steps are negative before repair and 38.40% of ranks are reordered; 36.88% of values change under sorting. Mean rank distance is 0.571 and maximum 30. Mean absolute sorting repair is 0.0007084 log units, maximum 0.68880. These crossings are material to the mechanism, even though average value adjustments are small. A low floor rate alone does not establish a mild operator.

The operator changes 6.59% of the full matrix before closure, with exact detection support. After closure, total gene variance ratio is 1.000619 and pseudobulk L2 shift 0.36445 versus v0096. All entries are finite/nonnegative and implied CP10k mass error is below 0.000676. No hidden target property is inferred from these diagnostics.
