## Source controls and interpretation

V0097_SOURCE_CONTROLS.json uses the same released E9.5 reference, all 32,285 genes and three frozen seeds for every arm. Robust shrinkage improves MMD, energy distance and variogram relative to exact v0096 in all three seeds. It also improves over the pooled-uncertainty control on every metric/seed. The unclosed local ablation has lower MMD than the closed candidate but worse energy distance; it is not a submission.

Critically, gene-permuted weights improve all three E9.5 discrepancies more than the gene-specific robust rule. This is a negative specificity control: the source comparison does not establish benefit from gene-specific reliability. Moving toward a source-stage carrier can improve source discrepancy without improving future prediction. The candidate is a descriptive uncertainty regularizer, not calibrated biological confidence or a validated future forecast. No parameter or eligibility rule was changed after these controls.

The isolated onset route was closed without a quota attempt. There were 17 eligible state-specific onset genes but zero globally new genes under independent-replicate and measurement-coverage requirements. This limited support does not establish that biological onsets are unimportant, and missing measured coverage was never treated as biological zero.
